🧠 Study & Focus Infographics

💡 1. Use Study Apps

Use apps like Notion, Google Keep, or Evernote to organize notes digitally.

💡 2. Stay Focused with Focus Mode

Use the Focus Mode feature on Android or iPhone to block distractions while studying.

💡 3. Learn with YouTube & AI

Watch educational videos and use AI tools like ChatGPT for concept clarity.

💡 4. Backup Your Notes

Save notes in Google Drive or Dropbox so you never lose your important files.

💡 5. Take Digital Breaks

Follow the 45-15 rule — study 45 minutes, then take a 15-minute screen-free break.
